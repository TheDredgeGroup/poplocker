Talk to Claude from your iPhone

You make a one-button Shortcut once, which takes about 3 minutes. After that you tap the button, talk, and your words are typed into the Claude chat on your PC. Below is a real picture of the finished shortcut, then a drawing for each step with the thing to tap circled in orange.

Before you start

  1. The iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.
  2. On the PC, open iPhone address.txt in the Pop Locker folder. Its second line is your shortcut line. It looks like http://192.168.1.20:5055/say?key=AbC1…&text=
  3. Get that whole line onto the iPhone. The easy way is to email it to yourself. Keep it private, because it has your key in it.

This is what you're making

The finished shortcut has just 4 steps, from top to bottom:

  1. Dictate text
  2. URL Encode Dictated Text
  3. Get contents of your shortcut line, with URL Encoded Text right after text=
  4. Speak Contents of URL

The address and key in the picture are made-up examples. Yours come from iPhone address.txt. The steps below show how to build it.

“make the summary shorter”

Tap the button, talk, and stop. Your phone says “Sent to Claude.” and Claude’s answer is read out on the PC.

If it says “Wrong key.”: paste the line again from iPhone address.txt. Five wrong keys lock the phone out for 15 minutes. If only your first word arrives: the last blue word in step 7 is Dictated Text when it must be URL Encoded Text. For the smart home: make a second shortcut the same way, with /home in place of /say in the line.
